About this system

This tool is the deployed component of an NTCC capstone project on diabetes risk prediction. It compares six model families — Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, and a custom ANN — trained on the Pima Indians Diabetes Database, and serves the best-performing one ({{ model_name }}) behind a Flask API.

What it is not

  • Not a clinical diagnostic tool, and not validated on real patients.
  • Not a substitute for a blood glucose test, HbA1c test, or a doctor's assessment.
  • Trained on 768 records from one historical population — predictions may not generalize across age, sex, or ethnicity.
