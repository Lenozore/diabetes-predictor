About

An end-to-end machine learning project exploring model selection and interpretability. I trained and compared six model families — Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, Random Forest, SVM, XGBoost, and a custom neural network — on the Pima Indians Diabetes Database to understand trade-offs between accuracy and generalization. The best-performing model ({{ model_name }}) is served via a Flask API.

Scope & Limitations

  • Not a clinical diagnostic tool — predictions are exploratory, not validated on real patients.
  • Not a substitute for blood glucose testing or a doctor's assessment.
  • Trained on 768 historical records from a single population — generalization across demographics is limited.
